# Spreadeagle: the spreadsheet, on GridCraft's engine (workstream T1, first half)

**`apps/spreadeagle`, `crates/sex-office`.** The first office app. `run spreadeagle NAME.xlsx` opens a workbook (xlsx, xls, csv, tsv); with no name it writes and opens `welcome.xlsx`.

- **Engine:** GridCraft's (github.com/storytold/gridcraft at c0ee120, MIT OR Apache-2.0), the ArtCraft suite's spreadsheet. Its formula engine, number formats, sheets, undo/redo and xlsx/xls/csv reader and writer build unchanged for `x86_64-sex-std.json`. It was compared with IronCalc (which also builds) and kept for its larger function library, xls and encrypted-OOXML reading, charts and macros. Size-optimised and stripped, the program is 12 MB (limit 16 MiB).
- **Window:** until egui runs on sex-app (M11) and GridCraft's own egui interface can be ported, `apps/spreadeagle/src/ui.rs` paints a grid with `sex-office` (Canvas + Pen: sex-font text with a glyph cache). It has a formula bar, column and row headings, text running into empty neighbours, numbers on the right, errors in red, sheet tabs, a status line, and mouse clicks and wheel.
- **Keys:** arrows, Tab, Page Up/Down and Ctrl+Home move. Typing replaces a cell, Enter edits it, Enter or Tab commits and Esc cancels. Delete clears. Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Ctrl+Page Up/Down change sheet and Ctrl+S saves.
- **Saving:** `std::fs::write` of the engine's bytes, not its write-then-rename, so a save works on every volume.
- **Tests:** `cargo test -p spreadeagle -p sex-office` (host): it opens the hand-written fixture (`apps/spreadeagle/make_welcome.py`), recalculates, undoes and redoes, round-trips xlsx and csv to xlsx, and checks text clipping. `scripts/program_spreadeagle_gate.py` (QEMU, in CI): open, edit B2, check the formulas recalculate, Ctrl+S, close, reopen and find the edit and the formulas kept, then change sheet.
- **Next:** open and save through Loom's picker, a selection range, copy and paste, column resize, then T2 (charts, sort and filter, freeze panes, ODS) and the egui port of GridCraft's interface once M11 lands.

# Moan Tapestry Pattern Workstation & Pro Studio DSP

**Core engine (`crates/moan-core`).** Adds Moan's `#![no_std]` Tapestry multi-channel pattern workstation and Pro Studio DSP suite at `48 kHz` (`RATE = 48_000`) with zero external dependencies:
- **Exact Musical Time & Tempo (`crates/moan-core/src/time.rs`)**: GCD-reduced rational `Time` (`MAX_DENOMINATOR = 15_360`, `DISPLAY_PPQ = 960`), compound `TimeSignature` (`4/4`, `3/4`, `5/4`, `6/8`, `7/8`), analytical `ln`/`exp` linear BPM ramp integration in `TempoMap`, and piecewise-linear global `Swing` (`0..=75%`).
- **Tapestry Workstation & Kits (`crates/moan-core/src/pattern.rs`, `crates/moan-core/src/kits.rs`)**: Shared `ChannelRack` (`<= 32` `RackChannel`s), beat-colored `16/32/64`-step grid (`1/16`, `1/12T`, `1/16T`, `1/32`, `1/64`, ratchet `1..=4`) unified directly over `ExpressiveNote` vectors, `Arc`-shared `TapestryPattern`s (`<= 256`) and linked `PatternInstance` clips (`<= 1024`) with `make_instance_unique` CoW forking and `split_pattern_by_channel`, `TransportContext::{PatternMode, SongMode}`, cross-channel `cut_group` choking (Closed Hat choking Open Hat), bar-quantized `8x8` `SceneMatrix`, `RetrospectiveNoteBuffer` (`<= 512` notes), `6` kit starters (`ALL_KITS`), and `6` genre song starters (`ALL_SONG_STARTERS`).
- **Expressive & Physical-Modeled Instruments (`crates/moan-core/src/instruments.rs`, `midi.rs`, `device_presets.rs`)**: `NoteExpr` / `InstrumentParams` API and 7 instruments (`5..=11`): `Sub808` (4x oversampled waveshaper, 16-frame zero-cross guard, legato pitch glides, formant growl; `14` presets), `DrumKit` (`Electronic`, `Circuit`, `ModalAcoustic`, GM choke; `10` presets), `TineKeys`, `ModalGrand`, `PluckGuitar` (Thiran fractional-delay waveguide + 2x oversampled `GuitarAmp` / 4x12 cab), `ModalMallet` (`Marimba`, `TubularBell`, `Vibraphone`), and `CrossSynth` (Cross-FM + hard sync + ring mod + TPT SVF).
- **Piano Roll Tools & SMF (`crates/moan-core/src/compose.rs`, `crates/moan-core/src/smf.rs`)**: `ExpressiveNote` with `slide_to_key` connectors and 10 deterministic `ComposeTool` transforms (`Legato`, `Articulate`, `Chop`, `Arpeggiate`, `Strum`, `Flam`, `Humanize`, `Levels`, `Flip`, `Tidy`); zero-dependency `#![no_std]` SMF Format 0/1 importer (`import_smf`, `to_tapestry_bank`) and Format 1 `960 PPQ` exporter (`export_smf_pattern`, `export_smf_midi_pattern`, `export_smf_bank`) capped at `4 MiB`, `32` tracks, and `4096` notes.
- **Pro Console Mixing & Mastering (`crates/moan-core/src/parametric_eq.rs`, `tape_fx.rs`, `mastering.rs`, `lib.rs`, `device_presets.rs`)**: 6-band RBJ `ParametricEq`, 256/512-point stereo-power radix-2 `SpectrumAnalyzer`, `detect_resonances` (`<= 3` narrow cuts capped at `-6 dB`), `SidechainCompressor`, `SidechainGate` (`5` presets), `TapeMachine` (6 ms modulated delay, 85 Hz head bump, wow/flutter, gated hiss; `6` presets), `TransientShaper`, `BitCrusher`, `StereoChorus`, topological `render_console_mix` (`<= 64` tracks, `SendRoute` post-fader aux sends, `output_bus`, `SidechainSource`, `MasterBus`), and `MasteringProcessor` with `4.0 ms` (`192`-frame) 2x inter-sample `LookaheadLimiter` (`8` presets).

**App UI, Persistence & Provenance (`apps/moan`).** `apps/moan/src/tapestry_ui.rs` (`Workspace::Tapestry`), `apps/moan/src/console_fx_ui.rs`, `apps/moan/src/piano.rs` (diagonal pitch-slide connectors), `apps/moan/src/project.rs` (`EXT5` persistence preserving byte-identical `v1`–`v4` files), and `apps/moan/src/project_io.rs` (`.mid` file import/export) wire Tapestry's Channel Rack, Pattern/Song modes, `Make Unique`, Kit & Song Starters, Scene Matrix, retrospective riff recovery, Piano Roll note tools, 7 new instruments, 6-band EQ + resonance auto-cut, sidechain dynamics, Tape, and 4 ms lookahead Mastering onto `moan-core`. Design informed by open-source research of `Vheissu/peel-studio` (`GPL-3.0-or-later`, compatible with SexOS `GPL-3.0-only`), with original `#![no_std]` implementations and Moan-native names/presets. Doc: `docs/moan/TAPESTRY_AND_DSP.md`.

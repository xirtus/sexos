## Focus, zoom and viewport paths

- SID205/SURFACE_ID_BROWSER (`main.rs:4610`) and Browser frame8 are the old WebStub placeholder. They are not actual Browser300 and must not be globally replaced with current Browser SID.
- Generic pointer focus and zoom operate on selected frame/surface. `toggle_zoom_frame:20468` rejects Closing/Tombstoned/Destroyed; zoom/unzoom update geometry and invoke native child/viewport handling. `launcher_focus_existing:16089–16091` explicitly excludes Browser. Do not silently enable future Browser launcher activation by deleting that exclusion; activation/focus custody needs its own phase.
- `DOCUMENT_VIEWS` at `main.rs:5342` has one Delivery per catalog document row, already includes Browser. `native_child_geometry:20273` queues held target/task and actual geometry. `document_view_poll:5372–5389` fixes route/frame per catalog row, not global current Browser, and transmits retained Notice; this is already safer than singleton retargeting.
- `document_view::Delivery` (`crates/sex-pdx/src/document_view.rs:135–210`) has a monotonic checked sequence and permanent retire. Full retains Notice, pending desired geometry may coalesce to a higher sequence, and hard error/live=false retires. However it accepts target/task anew on every queue and deduplicates only width/height. Never reuse one Delivery index for a new incarnation: same-size new target could be suppressed, or pending old geometry could be overwritten with a new task while route remains tied to another catalog row. Current distinct per-row allocation avoids that; constructor binding would make the invariant explicit.
- Viewport live checks currently rely on local custody flags and target association; unlike APP_INPUT they do not perform fresh exact catalog/info queries before every send. READY and OPEN_LINEN handlers (`main.rs:25000–25001`) compare caller PD and payload task but not kernel `caller_task`. This is an adjacent task-authentication gap to address or explicitly exclude from an input/viewport milestone; do not claim exact packet-task fencing from payload equality alone.

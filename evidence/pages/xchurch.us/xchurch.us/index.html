xChurch Believes in Time Travel

even if the technology remains stubbornly in the future.

Time is the degradation of matter, matter is inside spirit, therefor God exists beyond space time and everything within spacetime is immediately part of the eternal God. as soon as anything happens it is already part of the God from the beginning integrated. The universe is evolving towards a state of increasing complexity and unity. Humans are the culmination of the evolutionary process and play a unique role in God's plan for the universe. If we are going to perfect the timeline we should also expect butterfly effects to the timeline which could drastically change the course of history or even evolution, or all the choices that were necessary to lead to Christ's coming. Christ is the Omega Point of evolution, the goal towards which all creation is moving. The Holy Spirit is the energy that drives evolution forward.

Time travel could be achieved and it's most likely to be achieved by intelligent healthy people that come from prolific families, heightening the probability that we will have some play in time travel. We are called to collaborate with God in the work of creation and evolution. We should be aware of the presence of God in all of creation. We should be committed to justice and peace in the world. We should work to build a more united and fraternal world. We should be open to the new and the unexpected. We should be willing to risk and sacrifice ourselves for the sake of the common good. If we were to time travel, and to be able to effect history, and we know that religious deaths and wars are already common place, then religious prophets are ideal candidates for our visitation.

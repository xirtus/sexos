Issue 01 / An ocean of loose threads

PigalooArchipelago

Four living islands. One restless ocean. A sword, a stitch, and somewhere to go. You are a Restitcher, a wandering pixie who can hear the islands' failing rhythm, and you have four Heartstitches to restore.

Begin expedition

An early playable expedition. Keyboard, trackpad and mouse on a laptop or desktop. It is a big download the first time.

Where it begins

The Unravelling

A soft green-and-rose home island that gets stranger uphill, with a split chalk ridge threaded by one huge loose stitch. First sword lessons, first raft.

Climb and fight

Puffpaint Caldera

A broken crater rim pouring a rainbow waterfall into the jungle below. Vertical, loud and full of flowing pigment.

Learn the water

Mirecourt

A tilted crown of drowned terraces rising above reedwood. Patient duels and careful swims.

Everything at once

Wrongside Crown

A hooked snow ridge and a mountain split into canvas folds. Soft on the surface, very different inside.

    The story

    We were streaming in 1999.

    Netflix started in 2007. We're just saying.

    1. 1999

      Pigaloo goes online

      A streaming site, back when that was a ridiculous idea and the logo was a pig with wings. If pigs could fly, video could travel down a phone line.

    2. 2007

      Netflix starts streaming

      Eight years later. The pig remains gracious about it.

    3. 2026

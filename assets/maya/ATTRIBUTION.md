# Maya day signs: attribution

The twenty day-sign drawings in `day-signs/` are by **CJLL Wright**, from
Wikimedia Commons (the day-sign set shown on Wikipedia's *Maya calendar*
article, files `MAYA-g-log-cal-D01-Imix.png` ... `MAYA-g-log-cal-D20-Ajaw.png`),
licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 2.5**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/).

`day-signs.txt` and `svg/` are vector traces of those drawings, made by
`scripts/brand/maya_glyphs.py` (potrace), and are adaptations of them under the
same licence: credit CJLL Wright when you reuse them. The SexOS changes are the
tracing, the silhouette layer and the seal colours.

SexOS draws them at run time with `sex_icons::maya` (the calendar drop-down's
oracle cross and wave spell).

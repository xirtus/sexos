# Maya day signs: attribution

SexOS draws the twenty Maya day signs (the calendar drop-down's oracle cross
and wave spell, `sex_icons::maya`) from the `dreamroot-core` crate of the
Dreamroot calendar, https://github.com/xirtus/dreamroot, which SexOS depends
on instead of keeping its own copy.

The signs are vector traces of drawings by **CJLL Wright**, from Wikimedia
Commons (the day-sign set shown on Wikipedia's *Maya calendar* article, files
`MAYA-g-log-cal-D01-Imix.png` ... `MAYA-g-log-cal-D20-Ajaw.png`), licensed
under **Creative Commons Attribution 2.5**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/). The traces are adaptations
under the same licence: credit CJLL Wright when you reuse them. The original
drawings, the SVGs and the tracing script (`scripts/maya_glyphs.py`) live in
the Dreamroot repository, with its own copy of this credit in
`assets/maya/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
